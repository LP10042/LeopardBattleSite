Mission Brief

This is more than a tank battle.

The battlefield is no longer just about armor. It is about intelligence, strategy, and total battlefield domination. Command a powerful modern battle tank, then rise above the fight with your personal combat drone.

Scan terrain, acquire targets, and coordinate precision strikes from the air while your tank delivers devastating ground fire. Every encounter becomes a contest of awareness and tactical execution.

Core Pillar

Two battlefields. One war.

Ground Warfare

Your Tank

Drive powerful armored vehicles, maneuver through dangerous terrain, aim your cannon, and unleash devastating firepower against enemy forces.

  • Responsive tank handling & turret control
  • Shell and missile weapon systems
  • Clear health, cooldown & speed feedback
  • Scoped aiming with range markers
Aerial Warfare

Your Drone

Every multiplayer player commands a personal combat drone. Rise above the battlefield, locate hidden armor, and turn intelligence into decisive strikes.

  • Aerial reconnaissance & target tracking
  • Precision drone strikes
  • Reveal positions invisible from the ground
  • True combined-arms coordination
